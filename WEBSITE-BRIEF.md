# BOUY TRADERS — Website Master Prompt

Build and continuously improve a premium, modern, responsive corporate website for **BOUY TRADERS LOGISTICS & CARGO (PRIVATE) LIMITED**.

## Brand identity — follow the supplied original logo
Use the exact official company logo supplied by the client wherever the image asset is available. The emblem includes a golden eagle, globe, lighthouse, chain and shield. Never replace it with initials, a text-only mark, or an invented emblem. Place the original logo in the header, homepage hero, About section and footer.

Use a cohesive palette sampled from the logo:
- Deep navy blue for trust, structure and navigation
- Ocean teal for logistics and maritime trade
- Emerald green for trade, sourcing and agriculture
- Antique gold for premium accents, borders and calls to action
- Warm cream/ivory for clean backgrounds

Use refined typography, generous whitespace, strong visual hierarchy, restrained motion, premium photography and polished responsive layouts. Avoid clutter, generic template styling and inconsistent colors.

## Company profile
Present BOUY TRADERS as a Pakistan-based multi-category import-export and wholesale trading company. Do not invent addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, certifications, licenses, partnerships, authorised-dealer status, stock levels or export destinations.

## Business and product categories
1. **Fisheries & Seafood** — fish and seafood trading, wholesale supply and import/export enquiries.
2. **Rice Export & Wholesale** — rice exports, local wholesale, bulk supply and stock enquiries.
3. **Himalayan Pink Salt** — pink salt wholesale, bulk supply and export enquiries; allow enquiries about grade, packaging and quantity without making unsupported quality or origin certifications.
4. **Machinery & Instruments** — imported machinery, equipment and instruments for business requirements.
5. **Traditional Kashigari Crockery** — traditional crockery, tableware and household utensils.
6. **Branded Clothing & Fabrics** — wholesale clothing and fabric stock, including Blender's, Sapphire, J., R.O.D, Alkaram, Ideas and Gul Ahmed, subject to actual availability. Clearly state that trademarks belong to their respective owners and their mention does not imply official partnership or authorised-dealer status.
7. **General Import, Export & Wholesale Trading** — sourcing, distribution and business-to-business supply enquiries.

## Required website experience
- Premium homepage hero with the official logo and a clear company positioning statement
- About the company
- Business divisions and product catalogue with distinct, attractive product cards
- Dedicated Pink Salt product card and enquiry option
- Brand portfolio section with trademark disclaimer
- Import/export and wholesale enquiry process
- Warehouse/stock enquiry section without fabricated claims
- Contact and business enquiry form
- Professional footer with real contact details only when supplied

## Functional and technical requirements
- English-language website, mobile-first and responsive on phone, tablet and desktop
- Accessible semantic HTML, keyboard navigation and readable contrast
- Fast-loading optimised assets, SEO metadata, descriptive page titles and social sharing metadata
- Working mobile navigation and validated enquiry form
- Do not claim a form sends messages until a real company email/form endpoint has been configured and tested
- Keep code organised, maintainable and compatible with GitHub Pages from the repository root
- Use relative asset paths suitable for the GitHub Pages project URL
- Test links, navigation, layout breakpoints, image paths and form behaviour before declaring the site complete

## Delivery instruction
Implement the website in the existing GitHub repository, preserving the actual supplied company logo and all confirmed business categories. Make the site look like a credible, premium international trading company — not a generic logistics template. Never claim deployment or live functionality has been verified unless it has actually been checked.
